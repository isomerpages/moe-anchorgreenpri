The Talent Development Programme adopts a 3**Es approach (Excite, Enrich and Excel)** to provide a coherent and purposeful pathway for **Primary 3 to 6 students** to discover, develop and excel in their areas of strength.
Through a range of MOE-initiated and school-based programmes, Anchorlites are provided with enriching opportunities to spark curiosity, deepen passion and nurture their talents. These experiences support students in developing 21st-century competencies (21CC), including Critical, Adaptive and Inventive Thinking (CAIT), as well as Communication, Collaboration and Information (CCI) skills, equipping them to grow as confident and agile learners.

For students with identified academic strengths and talents, the school provides targeted opportunities for further development, supporting them in actualising their potential. These opportunities span the academic, sports and aesthetics domains, enabling students to pursue their interests more deeply while demonstrating curiosity, creativity, resilience and excellence.

For Primary 4 to 6 students, opportunities are provided across Languages, Mathematics and Science to support the balanced development of their academic strengths. Selected students may opt to participate in a Language TDP on Mondays and/or a Mathematics or Science TDP on Tuesdays. Each programme runs for one hour, from 3.00 p.m. to 4.00 p.m. Anchorlites with potential in arts may participate in Art Charts on Mondays from 3.00 p.m. to 5.00 p.m.
